Retro Arcade — Privacy Policy

Orbit Oryx · Last updated: August 13, 2026

Retro Arcade is a paid game collection with no ads and no tracking. This policy says everything we do with your data — it's short because we collect almost nothing.

What we collect

DataWhyWhere it lives
Player name (chosen by you)Shown on your records; there is no sign-up and no emailOur backend (Supabase)
Game high scoresSo your best scores follow you across devicesSupabase

That is the complete list. Your identity is anchored to your App Store / Google Play account through Apple Game Center / Google Play Games — Apple and Google handle that sign-in themselves; we never receive your email or password.

What we do NOT do

  • No ads, ever. There are no advertising networks or ad identifiers in the app.
  • No tracking or analytics. No analytics SDKs, advertising IDs, fingerprinting, or cross-app tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of data. Your data is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for their own purposes.
  • No data collection from the demo. Playing offline stores scores only on your device.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is processed entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. Purchase receipts are validated by RevenueCat solely to confirm your unlock; this uses an anonymous app user ID tied to your account.

Your rights

  • Delete your account any time in Settings → Delete my account. This permanently removes your player name and all scores from our servers, immediately.
  • Access or questions: email support@orbitoryx.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

We do not knowingly collect data from children beyond the same player name/scores above; no additional data is collected from any user regardless of age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we'll update this page and the "last updated" date. Given the app's design — no ads, no tracking, one-time purchase — we don't expect it to change much.
